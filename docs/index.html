Three ways in, one app

Enter any of these in Settings. You don't need all three. In automatic mode the app tests every address at once and uses the best one that answers.

Local

Straight to your server over home Wi‑Fi or Ethernet. Fastest, and works even when your internet is down.

http://homeassistant.local:8123

Used first when you're on Wi‑Fi or Ethernet.

Tailscale

A private, encrypted link to your home over your tailnet. Nothing exposed to the internet.

http://homeassistant.<tailnet>.ts.net:8123

Used when the Tailscale VPN is on.

Remote

Your public address, such as Home Assistant Cloud or your own domain behind HTTPS.

https://ha.example.com

Used when neither of the others answers.

How automatic mode decides

You can also lock it to one connection from the ⋮ menu → Connect via.

  1. Check every address at onceEach one gets a quick test, all in parallel, so you're not left waiting on a dead address.
  2. Pick the best one that answersLocal on Wi‑Fi, then Tailscale when it's on, then Remote, then anything else that responds.
  3. Watch for network changesLeave home, lose Wi‑Fi, or toggle Tailscale and it re-checks within seconds.
  4. Switch without losing your placeThe dashboard you had open reopens on the new address.

Set up Tailscale

Home Panel uses the Tailscale app you already have. It doesn't run a second VPN of its own.

  1. Put Home Assistant on your tailnetFor example with the Tailscale add-on for Home Assistant.
  2. Install Tailscale on your phoneSign in to the same tailnet. Get it on Google Play.
  3. Add the address in Home PanelSettings → Tailscale URL: the MagicDNS name, e.g. http://homeassistant.<tailnet>.ts.net:8123, or its 100.x.y.z address. If you use Tailscale's HTTPS, use https://homeassistant.<tailnet>.ts.net.

Questions

Why does the app allow plain http:// addresses?

Home Assistant on a home network is usually served over plain http, and traffic over Tailscale is already encrypted end to end. For an address on the public internet, use https.

It says Tailscale isn't connected, but it is.

Make sure Tailscale is routing this app's traffic. If you use Tailscale's per-app settings, include Home Panel. Then tap Reconnect in the ⋮ menu.

My local address stopped working on Android 17.

Android 17 asks before apps can reach devices on your home network. Allow it in system Settings → Apps → Home Panel → Permissions (“Nearby devices” / local network).

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. It connects only to the addresses you enter. There's no analytics and no account.
